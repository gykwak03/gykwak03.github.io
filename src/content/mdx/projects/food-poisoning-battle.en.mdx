**Food Poisoning Battle** is an educational Unity strategy game created for the
2023 Kyung Hee University KHUthon. The concept began with a norovirus awareness
campaign and asked how food-safety information could become part of a player’s
decisions. The game therefore translates pathogen growth conditions into resource
management, unit selection, and environmental-control mechanics.

Players accumulate amino acids over time and spend them to summon one of four
foodborne pathogens. *Vibrio parahaemolyticus* is a low-cost baseline unit,
*Staphylococcus aureus* applies additional toxin damage, norovirus attacks more
quickly and favors cold conditions, and *Clostridium perfringens* delivers high
damage. Each organism has a different cost, combat behavior, and preferred
environment. When the relevant growth conditions are satisfied, a single action
summons two units instead of one.

Environmental variables can be adjusted during play, requiring the player to
compare the conditions shown in contextual UI help with the current battle state.
The opposing side summons pathogens randomly, while both sides compete to destroy
the other colony. Four stage victories lead to an ending in which the surviving
pathogen enters food and harms a human host.

The completed prototype embeds food-safety knowledge in the strategic loop:
growth conditions affect the units a player can produce efficiently and therefore
change the outcome of the match. This structure connects pathogen characteristics
to observable gameplay consequences rather than presenting them only as static
reference text.
