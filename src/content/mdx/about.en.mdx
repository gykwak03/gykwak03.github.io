I am an undergraduate at Kyung Hee University double majoring in **Food Science
& Nutrition** and **Software Convergence**. I also studied Data Science &
Engineering as an exchange student at **Instituto Superior Técnico, University
of Lisbon**, taking courses in Deep Learning, Natural Language, Information
Systems and Databases, and Parallel & Heterogeneous Computing Systems.

At the **Integrative Research Center for Cerebrovascular and Cardiovascular Diseases, Yonsei University College of Medicine**, I led a study on
whether large language models can prioritize clinical variables for ICU delirium
prediction without access to patient outcomes. I constructed the MIMIC-IV cohort,
compared feature-selection procedures across three models, measured run-to-run
stability, and benchmarked the selected variables with downstream XGBoost models.

My other work includes a restaurant-site survival model built from 16 public
datasets, a two-stage dental image screening pipeline, a statistically evaluated
taste-preference survey, and a spatial analysis of book-delivery robot hubs.

I am interested in **healthcare AI, applied machine learning, and reliable data
analysis**, particularly research that connects domain knowledge with systems
that can be evaluated and used in practice.
